Northwind Community Library opens from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays and from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays.

Members may borrow up to five books at a time. The standard loan period is fourteen days. A loan can be renewed once when no other member has reserved the book.

The library does not charge daily late fees. An account is paused when an item is more than twenty-one days overdue, and borrowing resumes after the item is returned or replaced.

Study rooms can be reserved for two hours per day. Reservations must be cancelled at least one hour before the start time so another member can use the room.
